What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple calculation that uses your height and weight to estimate whether you're in a healthy weight range. It's widely used by doctors, fitness trainers, and health organizations worldwide.

How to Use This BMI Calculator

  1. Choose your preferred unit: Metric (kg/cm) or Imperial (lb/in)
  2. Enter your weight
  3. Enter your height
  4. Click Calculate BMI
  5. See your BMI value and category instantly

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, a healthy BMI is between 18.5 and 24.9.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI is a general guideline. It may not be accurate for athletes, pregnant women, elderly people, or children.
