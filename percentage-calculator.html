What is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator helps you compute percentages quickly and accurately. Whether you need to find what 20% of 500 is, or calculate the percentage increase between two numbers, this tool does it instantly.

How to Use

  1. Choose the type of calculation from the tabs
  2. Enter the values
  3. Click Calculate
  4. Get instant result

Common Percentage Formulas

  • X% of Y: (Y × X) ÷ 100
  • X is what % of Y: (X ÷ Y) × 100
  • Percentage Increase: ((New - Old) ÷ Old) × 100
  • Percentage Decrease: ((Old - New) ÷ Old) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage?

To find X% of Y: multiply Y by X, then divide by 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Percentage increase = ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100.
