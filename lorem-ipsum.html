What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy placeholder text used in design and publishing since the 1500s. It has a distribution of letters similar to English, making it look like real text while remaining meaningless. This lets designers and developers focus on layout, typography, and visual design without the distraction of readable content.

Why Use Lorem Ipsum?

  • Realistic appearance: Looks like natural text at a glance
  • Standard practice: Used by designers worldwide since 1500s
