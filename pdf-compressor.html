What is a PDF Compressor?

A PDF compressor reduces the file size of a PDF document. This is useful when you need to email a PDF, upload it to a website with size limits, or save storage space. Image-heavy PDFs (scans, presentations, brochures) see the biggest reduction.

How to Compress a PDF

  1. Upload a PDF file (drag & drop supported).
  2. Adjust the image quality slider (lower = smaller file).
  3. Click Compress PDF and download the result.

Features

  • Reduce file size: Up to 70% smaller for image-heavy PDFs.
  • Quality control: Choose your balance of size vs. quality.
  • No watermark: Clean output PDF.
  • 100% private: Everything runs in your browser.

When to Use a PDF Compressor

  • Email attachments: Most email services limit attachments to 25 MB.
  • Website uploads: Many portals have PDF size limits (2-10 MB).
  • Cloud storage: Save space in Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.
  • Faster sharing: Smaller files send and download quicker.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF compressor free?

Yes, 100% free with no watermark, no signup, and no limits.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. All compression happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

How much can I reduce PDF size?

Depending on the PDF, you can reduce file size by 30-70%. Image-heavy PDFs see the biggest reduction.

Will compression affect text quality?

No. Text remains sharp. Only images inside the PDF are re-compressed based on your quality setting.

Can I compress password-protected PDFs?

Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first. Remove the password in your PDF reader, then compress here.

Is there a file size limit?

No hard limit, but very large files (100 MB+) may be slow depending on your device. For best results, compress files under 50 MB.
