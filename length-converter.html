What is a Length Converter?

A length converter helps you convert between different units of length or distance. Whether you're working on a DIY project, doing homework, or dealing with international measurements, this tool makes conversions instant and accurate.

Common Length Conversions

  • 1 meter = 3.28084 feet = 39.3701 inches
  • 1 kilometer = 0.621371 miles = 1000 meters
  • 1 mile = 1.60934 kilometers = 5280 feet
  • 1 foot = 30.48 centimeters = 12 inches
  • 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters
  • 1 yard = 0.9144 meters = 3 feet

How to Use

  1. Enter the value you want to convert
  2. Select the "From" unit
  3. Select the "To" unit
  4. Click Convert
  5. Use the swap button (⇄) to reverse units
