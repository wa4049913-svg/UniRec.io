What is a Loan / EMI Calculator?

A loan calculator (also called EMI calculator) is a tool that tells you how much you'll pay every month when you take out a loan. EMI stands for "Equated Monthly Installment" — the fixed amount you pay each month until the loan is fully paid off.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter the loan amount (how much you're borrowing)
  2. Enter the annual interest rate (from your bank)
  3. Enter the loan term in years
  4. Click Calculate EMI
  5. See your monthly payment and total interest

The EMI Formula

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

  • P = Principal (loan amount)
  • R = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • N = Number of monthly installments (years × 12)

Tips to Reduce Loan Interest

  • Larger down payment: Borrow less, pay less interest
  • Shorter loan term: Pay more per month but less total interest
  • Better interest rate: Negotiate or improve credit score
  • Prepayment: Pay extra when possible to reduce principal

Frequently Asked Questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the formula: [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]. This tool does it automatically.

What's included in my EMI?

Your EMI includes both principal repayment and interest. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal.

Can I use this for any type of loan?

Yes. It works for home loans, car loans, personal loans, student loans, and business loans.
