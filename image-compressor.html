What is an Image Compressor?

An image compressor reduces the file size of your images by re-encoding them at a lower quality or converting them to more efficient formats. Smaller images load faster on websites, save storage space, and are easier to email or share.

How to Compress Images

  1. Upload one or more images (drag & drop supported).
  2. Set the quality slider (80% is a good balance).
  3. Choose output format: JPEG (smallest), WEBP (best), or PNG.
  4. Optionally set max width to resize large images.
  5. Click Compress Images and download the results.

Features

  • Batch compression: Compress multiple images at once.
  • Quality slider: From 10% (tiny) to 100% (original).
  • Format conversion: JPEG, WEBP, or PNG output.
  • Optional resize: Limit max width for even smaller files.
  • Live size savings: See how much each image shrank.
  • 100% private: All processing happens in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes, 100% free with no watermark, no signup, and no file limits.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All compression happens locally in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

Will compression reduce image quality?

At 80–90% quality, most images look identical to the original. Lower values give smaller files with more visible compression.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, and BMP are supported.
