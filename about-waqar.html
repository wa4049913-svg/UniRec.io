🚀 My Journey

I started my development journey with a simple goal: to create things that make people's lives easier. Over the years, I've worked on dozens of projects — from small websites to complex web applications and now indie games.

Screen recording always stood out as a problem that no one had solved properly. In 2026, while trying to record a quick tutorial for a friend, I realized there had to be a better way. That's when I started building Universal Screen Recorder Pro.

The goal was clear from the start:

  • Free forever — no subscriptions, no premium tiers
  • Browser-based — no installation, no downloads
  • Privacy-first — recordings never leave your device
  • No watermarks — clean, professional output
  • No time limits — record as long as you want

💡 My Philosophy

I believe that powerful creative tools shouldn't be locked behind paywalls. Everyone — whether a student in a small town or a professional content creator in a big city — deserves access to high-quality tools that help them create, share, and grow.

I also believe strongly in privacy. That's why all recording in Universal Screen Recorder Pro happens locally in your browser. Your videos never leave your device. No uploads, no cloud storage, no data collection. Just you and your recordings.
