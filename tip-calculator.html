What is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps you quickly figure out how much to tip at a restaurant, for delivery, or any service. It also splits the total bill (including tip) among multiple people — perfect for group dining.

How to Use

  1. Enter the bill amount
  2. Enter the tip percentage (or tap a quick tip button)
  3. Enter the number of people splitting the bill
  4. Click Calculate Tip
  5. See the total, tip, and per-person amount

Standard Tipping Guide

  • Restaurants (US): 15-20% for good service
  • Delivery: 10-15% (or $2-5 minimum)
  • Bartenders: $1-2 per drink
  • Hotel housekeeping: $2-5 per day
  • Taxi/Uber: 10-15%
  • Hair salon: 15-20%

Common Tip Examples

  • $50 bill, 15% tip: $57.50 total ($7.50 tip)
  • $100 bill, 20% tip: $120 total ($20 tip)
  • $200 bill, 18% tip, 4 people: $59 per person

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip?

In the US, 15-20% is standard at restaurants. 20% for excellent service, 10% for poor service.

Should I tip on pre-tax or post-tax?

Traditionally, tip on the pre-tax amount. But many people tip on the total for simplicity — the difference is small.

How do I split the bill with tip?

Add the tip to the bill, then divide by the number of people. This calculator does it automatically.
