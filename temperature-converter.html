What is a Temperature Converter?

A temperature converter helps you switch between temperature scales — Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin, and Rankine. Whether you're cooking, doing science homework, or checking international weather, this tool gives you instant accurate conversions.

Temperature Conversion Formulas

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • Celsius to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15
  • Kelvin to Celsius: °C = K − 273.15
  • Fahrenheit to Kelvin: K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15
  • Kelvin to Fahrenheit: °F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Common Temperature References

  • 0°C = 32°F (water freezes)
  • 100°C = 212°F (water boils)
  • 37°C = 98.6°F (body temperature)
  • 25°C = 77°F (room temperature)
  • Absolute Zero = -273.15°C = -459.67°F = 0 K

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. Formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32, multiply by 5, divide by 9. Formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.

What is absolute zero?

Absolute zero is 0 Kelvin = -273.15°C = -459.67°F — the coldest possible temperature.
