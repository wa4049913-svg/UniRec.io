What is a PDF Merger?

A PDF merger combines two or more PDF files into a single document. Useful for joining scanned pages, reports, invoices, presentations, or splitting-then-rejoining PDFs.

How to Merge PDFs

  1. Upload two or more PDF files (drag & drop supported).
  2. Reorder files by dragging or using the ↑ ↓ buttons.
  3. Click Merge PDFs and download the combined file.

Features

  • Unlimited files: Merge 2, 10, or 100 PDFs at once.
  • Reorder: Drag or use ↑ ↓ to arrange order.
  • Remove: Delete any file before merging.
  • No watermark: Output PDF is clean.
  • 100% private: Files stay in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF merger free?

Yes, 100% free with no watermark, no signup, and no page limits.

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. All merging happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first. Remove the password in your PDF reader, then merge here.

Is there a file size limit?

No hard limit, but very large files (100 MB+) may be slow depending on your device.
