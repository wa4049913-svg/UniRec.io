What is a Currency Converter?

A currency converter helps you convert money from one currency to another using live exchange rates. Whether you're traveling, shopping online internationally, or sending money abroad, this tool gives you instant conversions with accurate rates.

How to Use

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert
  2. Select the "From" currency
  3. Select the "To" currency
  4. Click Convert Currency
  5. Use the swap button (⇄) to reverse currencies

Exchange Rate Sources

This converter uses official central bank rates from around the world, updated daily. These rates are aggregated from multiple financial institutions and are the same rates used by banks worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often are exchange rates updated?

Rates are updated daily from official central banks. For real-time interbank rates, you'd need a premium API.

Is this converter free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no API key, no limits.

How accurate are the rates?

The rates come from official central banks — the same rates used by banks. However, actual exchange services may charge fees or use different rates.

Which currencies are supported?

200+ world currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, PKR, INR, AED, SAR, and more.
