What is a Duplicate Line Remover?

A duplicate line remover is an online tool that finds and deletes repeated lines from your text. It keeps only the unique lines and gives you a clean, deduplicated result. Perfect for cleaning lists, email dumps, CSV data, log files, keyword lists, and more.

How to Use the Duplicate Line Remover

  1. Paste or type your text into the box above (one item per line).
  2. Choose options like trim spaces, remove empty lines, or case-insensitive matching.
  3. Click Remove Duplicates to keep only unique lines.
  4. Use Sort, Reverse, or Shuffle to reorder lines.
  5. Copy the result or download it as a .txt file.

Features

  • Remove Duplicates: Keep only the first (or last) occurrence of each line.
  • Unique Only: Same as dedupe, but always keeps the first occurrence.
  • Trim Spaces: Remove extra spaces from the start and end of every line.
  • Remove Empty Lines: Delete blank lines automatically.
  • Case-Insensitive: Treat "Hello" and "hello" as the same line.
  • Sort A-Z / Z-A: Alphabetically sort the remaining lines.
  • Reverse: Flip the order of lines top-to-bottom.
  • Shuffle: Randomly reorder lines.

Common Use Cases

  • Keyword Lists: Deduplicate SEO keyword lists before use.
  • Email Lists: Remove repeated email addresses.
  • Log Files: Strip duplicate log entries.
  • Data Cleaning: Clean CSV-style data with one item per line.
  • To-Do Lists: Remove accidentally repeated items.
  • Developers: Deduplicate import statements, IDs, or URLs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does this tool do?

It removes duplicate lines from your text and keeps only the unique lines. You can also trim spaces, remove empty lines, sort, reverse, and shuffle lines.

Is this tool free?

Yes, this duplicate line remover is 100% free with no character limits, no signup, and no hidden charges.

Yes. Everything happens locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded or stored on any server.

Does it handle case-sensitive duplicates?

Yes. You can choose case-sensitive or case-insensitive deduplication. Case-insensitive treats "Hello" and "hello" as the same line.

Yes. The tool is fully responsive and works on Android, iPhone, iPad, tablets, and desktop browsers.
