What is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps you find the final price of a product after a percentage discount. It also shows how much money you saved. This is handy during sales, when comparing deals, or when you want to check if a discount is really worth it.

How to Use

  1. Enter the original price
  2. Enter the discount percentage
  3. Click Calculate Discount
  4. See the final price and your savings

Discount Formula

  • Final Price: Original Price × (1 − Discount% ÷ 100)
  • Savings: Original Price × (Discount% ÷ 100)

Common Discount Examples

  • 10% off $50: $45 (save $5)
  • 20% off $100: $80 (save $20)
  • 30% off $200: $140 (save $60)
  • 50% off $80: $40 (save $40)

Stacked Discounts — Important

If a store offers "20% off, then extra 10% off", the discounts stack on the already-reduced price. So 20% + 10% is not 30% — it's actually 28% total. This calculator handles single discounts only.
