What is a Time Zone Converter?

A time zone converter helps you find the exact time in another city or country. It's essential for scheduling international meetings, calling friends or family abroad, or planning travel across time zones.

How to Use

  1. Select the date and time you want to convert
  2. Select the From Time Zone
  3. Select the To Time Zone
  4. Click Convert Time
  5. See the converted time plus the difference

Common Time Zone Differences

  • Pakistan (PKT) to USA (EST): -10 hours
  • Pakistan (PKT) to UK (GMT): -5 hours
  • Pakistan (PKT) to UAE (GST): -1 hour
  • Pakistan (PKT) to India (IST): +0.5 hours
  • Pakistan (PKT) to China (CST): +3 hours

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool handle daylight saving time?

Yes. The converter automatically accounts for daylight saving time in both time zones based on the date you select.

How many time zones are supported?

All major time zones worldwide — covering every country and most major cities.

Is this tool free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup required.
