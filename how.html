How to Use Universal Screen Recorder Pro

A Complete Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners

Universal Screen Recorder Pro is designed to be the easiest screen recording tool you'll ever use. No downloads, no installations, no complicated settings — just open the website and start recording. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from your first recording to advanced tips for getting the best quality.

🎬 Quick Start (30 Seconds)

In a hurry? Here's the fastest way to record your screen:

  1. Open unirecorder.online
  2. Click the 🔴 Start Recording button
  3. Choose what to record (Entire Screen / Window / Browser Tab)
  4. Click Share in the popup
  5. Click ⏹️ Stop Recording when finished
  6. Click 📥 Download Video to save your file

That's it! You've recorded your screen in under a minute.

📖 Detailed Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Open the Website

Navigate to unirecorder.online using any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. The tool works on Windows, macOS, Linux, and ChromeOS without any installation.

Step 2: Configure Your Recording Settings

Before you start recording, take a moment to configure your settings. This ensures you get the quality and audio you need:

🎧 Audio Sources

  • Microphone — Your Voice: Enable this if you want to narrate your recording. Perfect for tutorials, gaming commentary, or presentations.
  • System Audio — Game/App Sound: Enable this to capture the sounds coming from your computer — game audio, video sound, notifications, etc.

💡 Tip: You can enable both at the same time. The tool will mix them together automatically.

🎬 Video Settings

  • Resolution:
    • 480p (SD): Smallest file size — best for quick shares
    • 720p (HD): Balanced quality and size — good for meetings
    • 1080p (Full HD): High quality — ideal for YouTube
    • 2K (2560x1440): Premium quality — best for gaming
    • 4K (3840x2160): Ultra HD — for professional content
  • Frame Rate:
    • 30 FPS: Standard — good for tutorials and meetings
    • 60 FPS: Ultra smooth — best for gaming and fast-motion content

📷 Webcam Overlay

  • Position: Choose from Top Right, Top Left, Bottom Right, or Bottom Left
  • Size: Small (160px), Medium (220px), or Large (300px)

⚙️ Advanced Tools

  • Playback Speed: Adjust the preview playback speed (0.5x to 2x). Doesn't affect the saved file — only the preview.

Step 3: Start Recording

Click the big 🔴 Start Recording button. A browser popup will appear asking what you want to record:

  • Entire Screen: Records everything on your monitor. Best for full demos.
  • Application Window: Records only a specific app (e.g., a game, Zoom, or your browser).
  • Browser Tab: Records only the current tab. Best for web tutorials.

Select your choice and click Share. The timer will start counting immediately.

💡 Tip: If you enabled "System Audio" earlier, make sure to check the "Share audio" checkbox in the popup. Otherwise, system audio won't be recorded.

Step 4: Control Your Recording

While recording, you have three controls:

  • ⏸️ Pause: Temporarily stop recording. The timer pauses too. Click again to resume.
  • ⏹️ Stop: End the recording completely. Your video becomes available for download.
  • Menu (⋮): Access settings and pages from the sidebar.

Step 5: Preview and Download

After clicking Stop, your recording will appear as a video preview below the controls. You can:

  • ▶️ Play the recording to verify it looks good
  • ⏩ Adjust playback speed if you selected a different speed
  • 📥 Click Download Video to save it to your device

A short 5-second countdown will appear before the download starts. Your file will be saved as a WebM file with the name format UniRec_YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS.webm.

🎯 Use Cases & Recommended Settings

🎮 Recording Gameplay

  • Resolution: 1080p or 2K
  • Frame Rate: 60 FPS
  • Audio: System Audio ON, Microphone ON (for commentary)
  • Tip: Close background apps before recording 2K to avoid lag.

🎓 Recording Tutorials

  • Resolution: 1080p
  • Frame Rate: 30 FPS
  • Audio: Microphone ON (narrate your steps)
  • Tip: Use a good external microphone for clear voice quality.

💼 Recording Meetings (Zoom, Google Meet)

  • Resolution: 720p or 1080p
  • Frame Rate: 30 FPS
  • Audio: System Audio ON, Microphone ON
  • Tip: Record only the browser tab if you don't want other windows visible.

📹 Recording YouTube or Browser Content

  • Resolution: 720p
  • Frame Rate: 30 FPS
  • Audio: System Audio ON
  • Tip: ⚠️ Only record content you have rights to. Do not record copyrighted material.

💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

  • Use Chrome or Edge: They offer the best compatibility and performance.
  • Close unnecessary tabs: Free up system resources before recording 2K or 60FPS.
  • Use headphones: Prevents echo when recording microphone + system audio together.
  • Test first: Do a 10-second test recording to verify audio and video quality.
  • Good lighting: If recording webcam, ensure proper lighting for professional results.
  • Don't record in 2K if you don't need it: 1080p is plenty for most uses and saves storage.
  • Check your mic: Speak clearly and at a moderate pace for best narration.

❓ Troubleshooting Common Issues

Screen recording option is not showing up

Make sure you're using a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari). Also, check that you're accessing the site via HTTPS — screen recording requires a secure connection.

No audio in the recording

Two things to check: (1) Make sure "System Audio" is checked before starting, and (2) In the browser popup, make sure you checked the "Share audio" option. For microphone, ensure your device's mic permission is granted.

Recording lags or stutters

This usually means your system is overloaded. Try: (1) Lower the resolution to 720p, (2) Use 30 FPS instead of 60, (3) Close other heavy applications, (4) Close unused browser tabs.

Recording stops automatically

This can happen if you accidentally close the browser tab, click "Stop Sharing" in the browser bar, or if the browser runs out of memory. Try recording in shorter segments.

Video file is too large

WebM files can get large for long recordings. To reduce file size: (1) Use lower resolution, (2) Use 30 FPS instead of 60, (3) Keep recordings shorter and combine later.

Can't download the video

Make sure you completed a recording first (the download button only appears after recording). If the issue persists, try refreshing the page and recording again.

🔒 Privacy & Security

All recording happens 100% locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Your videos are only saved to your device when you click the download button. This is one of the biggest advantages of using Universal Screen Recorder Pro compared to other online tools.
