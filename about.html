About Universal Screen Recorder Pro

Free, Fast, and Privacy-First Screen Recording for Everyone

Our Story

Universal Screen Recorder Pro was born out of a simple frustration — most screen recording tools are either too expensive, require heavy software downloads, or come loaded with watermarks and time limits. In 2026, while trying to record a quick tutorial for a friend, our founder Waqar Ahmed realized there was a real need for a lightweight, browser-based solution that "just works."

That's when the idea for Universal Screen Recorder Pro took shape. The goal was clear: build a screen recorder that runs entirely in the browser, requires no installation, has no watermarks, and respects user privacy above all else.

Today, Universal Screen Recorder Pro is used by thousands of gamers, teachers, students, content creators, and professionals around the world who need a reliable screen recorder without the usual hassle.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to make high-quality screen recording accessible to everyone, everywhere, for free. We believe that powerful creative tools shouldn't be locked behind paywalls or require technical expertise. Whether you're a student recording a class project, a gamer capturing epic moments, or a professional preparing a business presentation, you deserve a tool that works instantly and reliably.

What Makes Us Different?

  • 100% Browser-Based: No software to download, no installation, no admin permissions needed.
  • Privacy-First Design: All recordings happen locally in your browser. Your videos never leave your device.
  • No Watermarks: Your recordings come out clean and professional — no branding stamped on them.
  • No Time Limits: Record for as long as you need, whether it's 30 seconds or 3 hours.
  • Completely Free: No hidden fees, no premium tiers, no credit card required.
  • High-Quality Output: Up to 4K resolution at 60FPS for ultra-smooth recordings.
  • Webcam Overlay: Add your face cam with position and size controls.
  • Cross-Platform: Works on Windows, macOS, Linux, and ChromeOS with Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

Key Features

  • 🎥 Record entire screen, specific window, or browser tab
  • 🎤 Microphone and system audio recording simultaneously
  • 📷 Webcam overlay with 4 positions and 3 sizes
  • ⚙️ Customizable resolution (480p, 720p, 1080p, 2K, 4K)
  • 🎞️ Frame rate options (30 FPS and 60 FPS)
  • ⏸️ Pause and resume recording anytime
  • 📥 Instant local download — no uploads, no waiting
  • 📱 Mobile-friendly responsive design

Our Commitment to Privacy

Privacy is not an afterthought — it's built into the core of Universal Screen Recorder Pro. Unlike cloud-based recording tools that require you to upload your videos to their servers, our tool processes everything locally in your browser. We have no access to your recordings, and we never will.

We use industry-standard technologies like the HTML5 Screen Capture API to ensure your recording sessions are private and secure. Learn more about how we handle data in our Privacy Policy.

How We Keep It Free

Universal Screen Recorder Pro is and will always be free to use. To sustain the project and cover server costs, we display non-intrusive advertisements through Google AdSense and other advertising partners. These ads allow us to keep the tool free for everyone without compromising on quality or privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Universal Screen Recorder Pro really free?

Yes, 100% free with no hidden costs, subscriptions, or premium tiers. Every feature is available to every user.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start recording immediately without any registration, login, or personal information.

Where are my recordings stored?

Your recordings are stored temporarily in your browser's memory and are downloaded directly to your device. We never see or store them.

Can I use this tool for commercial purposes?

Yes, you can use recordings made with our tool for personal or commercial purposes. However, you are responsible for ensuring you have the right to record any content.

Will you ever charge for this tool?

Our core recording features will always remain free. We may introduce optional premium features in the future, but the free tier will not be discontinued.
