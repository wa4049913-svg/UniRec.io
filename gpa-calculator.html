What is a GPA Calculator?

A GPA calculator computes your Grade Point Average — the number that represents your overall academic performance. It converts each letter grade (A, B, C, etc.) into grade points, multiplies by credit hours, and averages them.

How to Use

  1. Enter your course name
  2. Select the grade you received (A, B, C, etc.)
  3. Enter the credit hours for that course
  4. Click Add Another Course to add more
  5. Click Calculate GPA

GPA Scale (4.0 System)

  • A / A+: 4.0 (Excellent)
  • A-: 3.7
  • B+: 3.3
  • B: 3.0 (Good)
  • B-: 2.7
  • C+: 2.3
  • C: 2.0 (Average)
  • C-: 1.7
  • D: 1.0 (Poor)
  • F: 0.0 (Fail)

What is a Good GPA?

  • 3.5 - 4.0: Excellent — top of class
  • 3.0 - 3.4: Good — above average
  • 2.5 - 2.9: Average — acceptable
  • 2.0 - 2.4: Below average — warning
  • Below 2.0: Academic probation risk

Frequently Asked Questions

How is GPA calculated?

GPA = Sum of (Grade Points × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA is for one semester. CGPA is the cumulative average of all semesters combined.

Can I calculate a weighted GPA?

This calculator uses the standard 4.0 unweighted scale. For weighted GPA, add 0.5 or 1.0 for honors/AP courses as per your school's policy.

Disclaimer: This calculator uses the standard 4.0 unweighted scale. Check your school's policy for exact grading.
