What is a Data Size Converter?

A data size converter helps you convert between different units of digital information — bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes, terabytes, and even bits. It's essential for understanding file sizes, storage capacities, and download speeds.

Data Size Units Explained

  • Bit (b): Smallest unit — 0 or 1
  • Byte (B): 8 bits — one character
  • Kilobyte (KB): 1024 bytes
  • Megabyte (MB): 1024 KB — a photo
  • Gigabyte (GB): 1024 MB — a movie
  • Terabyte (TB): 1024 GB — a hard drive
  • Petabyte (PB): 1024 TB — data center storage

Common Conversions

  • 1 KB = 1024 bytes
  • 1 MB = 1024 KB = 1,048,576 bytes
  • 1 GB = 1024 MB = 1,048,576 KB
  • 1 TB = 1024 GB = 1,048,576 MB
  • 1 PB = 1024 TB

Binary vs Decimal

This converter uses binary (1024-based) conversions — the traditional computing standard. Note that storage manufacturers sometimes use decimal (1000-based) values, which is why a "500 GB" drive often shows as ~465 GB on your computer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many MB in a GB?

1 GB = 1024 MB (binary standard).

How many GB in a TB?

1 TB = 1024 GB.

Why does my 500 GB drive show less space?

Drive manufacturers use decimal (1000-based), while operating systems use binary (1024-based). So 500 GB advertised = ~465 GiB actual.
