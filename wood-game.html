🌲 What is Wood?

Wood is an upcoming 3D first-person horror escape game developed by Waqar Games (WG), an independent solo studio from Pakistan. Built in Godot 4.2 with GDScript.

The core idea is simple and terrifying: a horror house hidden inside a store. What looks like a normal shop on the outside hides something much darker inside. You enter — and you don't come out until you escape.

Wood launches on PC first in 2027-2028, with a mobile version (Android & iOS) releasing after the PC launch. It is being developed entirely by Waqar Ahmed — solo.

📖 The Story — Trapped in the Horror House

It starts like any normal evening. You walk into a store — nothing special about it. Shelves. Dim lights. The kind of place you'd pass a hundred times without a second thought.

But at the back of the store, there's a door you've never noticed before.
And behind that door... a horror house.

Curiosity pulls you in. The moment you step through, the door behind you locks. The lights flicker. And somewhere deep inside the horror house, something moves.

You're not a ghost hunter. You're not a hero. You're just someone who walked into the wrong place at the wrong time — and now you have to find a way out.

The horror house isn't just a haunted attraction. It's alive. It knows you're inside. And the entities that live here — the ghosts, the bhoot — they don't want you to leave.

To escape, you must:

  • Explore the horror house: Every room hides a clue. Every hallway leads somewhere — or someone.
  • Survive the ghosts: They roam. They listen. Some of them are always watching.
  • Solve the puzzles: Locked doors, hidden keys, cursed objects — all part of the escape.
  • Uncover the truth: Why is there a horror house inside a store? Who built it? And why won't it let you go?

The full story is being written alongside development. More will be revealed closer to release. But one thing is certain — this horror house wasn't built by accident.

🎮 Gameplay — Escape the Horror House

👻 Survival Horror Inside a Cursed House

Wood is built around tension, fear, and escape. The horror house is your prison. The ghosts are your wardens. The only way out is through.

  • First-person 3D horror: Explore a claustrophobic horror house room by room.
  • Escape-based objectives: Find keys, unlock doors, and escape each section of the house.
  • Ghost encounters: Terrifying ghosts roam the horror house — some chase you, some hide, some wait.
  • Stealth & hiding: Hide under beds, inside closets, behind furniture. Stay silent.
  • Puzzle-solving: Unlock doors, decode symbols, find hidden passages.
  • Limited resources: Flashlight batteries, matches, keys — manage them carefully.
  • Dynamic scares: The house reacts to you. The more noise you make, the faster they find you.
  • Story-driven progression: Each room reveals a piece of the horror house's dark history.

👻 Ghosts & Entities of the House

The Things That Live Inside

The ghosts in Wood aren't just jump-scares. They're the heart of the horror — each with a unique behavior, sound, and reason for existing inside the horror house.

  • Wandering Ghosts: They roam the hallways. They don't see you — but they hear you.
  • Stalkers: They follow at a distance. You won't know they're there until it's too late.
  • The Watcher: It never moves when you look. It only moves when you don't.
  • Room Guardians: Certain ghosts guard specific rooms. Getting past them requires planning.
  • Unnamed Entities: Some things in the horror house aren't ghosts. And they aren't friendly.

Full details on each entity will be revealed in the months leading up to release. Follow Waqar Games for updates.
