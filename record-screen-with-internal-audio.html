Record your screen with internal audio, free and online

Want to capture the sound that plays on your computer (a video, a game, a call) along with your screen? You can do it right in your browser.

Open the free screen recorder

How to do it

  1. Open UniRecorder and click the start button.
  2. Choose what to share: a Chrome tab, a window, or the entire screen.
  3. Turn on the share audio option in the browser popup. On Windows, tab audio and entire-screen audio both work in Chrome and Edge.
  4. Record, then stop and download your video.
Start recording now

Common questions

Why is there no sound in my recording?

You probably forgot to tick the share audio checkbox in the browser popup. Stop, start again and tick it.

Does it work on Mac?

Chrome on Mac can capture tab audio. System-wide audio on Mac usually needs a virtual audio driver.
