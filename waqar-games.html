💪 The Real Story

From a Broken Laptop to Building an Upcoming Game 2027

Let me tell you the truth — the real story behind Waqar Games. It's not a story of a big studio with a huge budget, a powerful PC, and a full team. It's the opposite.

I'm building Wood on a broken, low-spec laptop with no GPU. It doesn't have an SSD — it runs on an old HDD. It takes 10-15 minutes just to boot up. Every task I do — running a game engine, rendering 3D models, testing code — comes with lag and struggle.

I don't have a proper home setup. I work from school, or a local center, with friends around me. In a situation where most people would give up, I chose to keep going.

While my friends are playing games, I'm building one. While others are watching videos, I'm modeling 3D objects, writing code, and pushing through lag. I chose the harder path — because I believe in what I'm building.

I spent 2 years on YouTube before this — trying to build something, only to realize the model I was following was broken for me. It wasn't the right path. So I switched. I picked up game development. And I haven't looked back since.

I've been working on this for years now. Every minute of lag. Every crash. Every failure. Every "why am I doing this?" moment — I've faced them all. But I'm still here. Still building. Still pushing.

My goal is simple: to become successful doing what I love. To prove that you don't need the best equipment or the biggest budget — you just need the will to keep going.

🛠️ How We Build Games

At Waqar Games, we handle every aspect of game development ourselves:

  • Game Design: Concept, story, mechanics, levels, and gameplay systems
  • Programming: Game logic, physics, AI, and optimization
  • 3D Modeling: Characters, environments, props, and everything in the game world
  • Texturing: Materials, surfaces, and visual details
  • Animation: Character animations, cutscenes, and dynamic effects
  • Sound Design: Music, sound effects, and audio atmosphere

It's a massive undertaking for one person — but it's what makes Waqar Games special. Every pixel, every line of code, every sound — it all comes from one developer with a dream.
