How to Record Screen on Windows 11 for Free (No Watermark)

By Waqar Ahmed · September 23, 2026 · 7 min read

Recording your screen on Windows 11 doesn't have to cost money. Whether you're creating tutorials, recording gameplay, or capturing meetings, there's a completely free way to do it — no watermark, no time limit, no download.

This guide shows you exactly how to record your screen on Windows 11 using UniRecorder, a free browser-based screen recorder.

💡 Quick Answer: Open unirecorder.online in Chrome or Edge → Click Start Recording → Select screen/window → Stop → Download. That's it.

What You'll Need

  • Windows 11 PC (any version)
  • Browser: Chrome, Edge, or Firefox
  • Internet: Only for loading the page (recording works offline)

Method 1: UniRecorder (Recommended — Free, No Watermark)

UniRecorder is a browser-based screen recorder. No download, no signup, no watermark. Here's how to use it:

1 Open UniRecorder in Chrome or Edge

Go to unirecorder.online. Chrome and Edge work best for screen recording on Windows 11.

2 Choose Your Video Settings

Select resolution (1080p or 720p for weak PCs) and FPS (30 or 60). For most tutorials, 1080p at 30 FPS is perfect.

3 Enable Audio Sources

Check Microphone for your voice and System Audio for game/app sound. You can enable both at the same time.

4 Enable Webcam (Optional)

If you want a face cam overlay, check "Include Webcam" and choose its position and size.

5 Click "Start Recording"

A browser popup will appear. Choose Entire Screen, Window, or Tab. Make sure to check "Share audio" if you want system sound.

6 Stop Recording

Click the red Stop Recording button or use the browser's "Stop Sharing" bar at the bottom.

7 Download, Edit, or Share

Preview your recording, trim the start/end, then download as WebM. You can also generate a share link.

✅ Done! Your recording is saved locally on your PC. No watermark, no upload, no limits.

Method 2: Xbox Game Bar (Built-in, Limited)

Windows 11 has a built-in recorder called Xbox Game Bar:

  1. Press Win + G to open Game Bar
  2. Click the Record button (or press Win + Alt + R)
  3. Stop with Win + Alt + R
  4. Files saved to Videos → Captures

⚠️ Xbox Game Bar Limitations:

  • ❌ Cannot record File Explorer or the desktop
  • ❌ No webcam overlay
  • ❌ Records one window at a time
  • ❌ Uses more system resources

Method 3: OBS Studio (Advanced, Requires Download)

OBS Studio is a free, open-source recorder for advanced users. It supports scenes, sources, and streaming.

Downside: Steeper learning curve, requires installation, and heavier on system resources.

Tips for Smooth Recording on Windows 11

  • Close unused tabs before recording 4K or 60 FPS
  • Use 720p at 30 FPS on low-end PCs to avoid lag
  • Enable Do Not Disturb to hide notifications
  • Plug in your laptop — battery mode throttles CPU
  • Use headphones to avoid echo when recording mic + system audio
  • Test 10 seconds before starting a long recording

How do I record my screen on Windows 11 for free?

Yes. Windows 11 has Xbox Game Bar (Win + G) which can record your screen. However, it cannot record File Explorer or the desktop, and requires a lot of system resources.

Can I record my screen with audio on Windows 11?

Yes. UniRecorder can record system audio (game/app sound) and microphone (your voice) simultaneously.

Is there a free screen recorder with no watermark for Windows 11?

Yes. UniRecorder is completely free with no watermark, no time limit, and no signup required.

How do I record my screen on Windows 11 without downloading software?

Use a browser-based recorder like UniRecorder. It runs entirely in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox — no download, no installation.

Why is my screen recording laggy on Windows 11?
