Frequently Asked Questions

Is this online screen recorder really free?

Yes. Universal Screen Recorder Pro is 100% free with no hidden charges, no time limits, and no watermark on your recordings.

How do I record my screen with audio?

Enable both "Microphone" and "System Audio" checkboxes before starting. In the browser popup, make sure "Share audio" is checked.

Why is my recording laggy?

Lag happens when your PC is overloaded. Try: lowering resolution to 720p, using 30 FPS instead of 60, closing background apps.

Can I use this without downloading software?

Yes. Universal Screen Recorder Pro runs entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Screen Capture API.

Is there a time limit on recordings?

No. You can record for as long as you want. The only limit is your device storage.

Can I record copyrighted content?

No. Recording copyrighted content without permission may violate copyright laws. Only record content you own or have permission to record.

How do I reduce file size?

Use 720p instead of 1080p, use 30 FPS instead of 60, keep recordings shorter, or compress afterward.

What is WebM format?

WebM is an open-source video format developed by Google. Supported by YouTube, Chrome, Firefox, and most modern players.

Can I pause and resume recording?

Yes. Click Pause during recording and Resume to continue. The timer also pauses and resumes.

Why does the preview look lower quality?

The preview is scaled down by the browser for faster playback. Once you download the file, you get the full original quality.

Can I record with webcam overlay?

Yes. Enable the Webcam option, choose position and size.

Are my recordings uploaded to any server?

No. All recording and processing happens locally in your browser.
