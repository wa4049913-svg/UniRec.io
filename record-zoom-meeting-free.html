How to record a Zoom or Google Meet call for free

If you are not the host, you often can't use Zoom's built-in recording. A screen recorder works for any meeting app. Always tell people you are recording.

How to do it

  1. Open UniRecorder in one tab and join your meeting in another.
  2. Start recording and choose the meeting tab or window.
  3. Enable audio sharing so you capture everyone's voice.
  4. Stop when the meeting ends and download the video.
