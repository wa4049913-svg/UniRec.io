What is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer changes the width and height of your images in pixels or percent. Resizing is essential for web images, social media posts, profile pictures, thumbnails, and reducing file size before uploading.

How to Resize Images

  1. Upload one or more images (drag & drop supported).
  2. Choose a mode: Pixels, Percent, or Preset.
  3. Enter width/height, or pick a preset like Instagram or Facebook OG.
  4. Optionally keep aspect ratio to avoid stretching.
  5. Click Resize Images and download the results.

Features

  • Resize by pixels: Any width × height.
  • Resize by percent: Scale up or down (1–500%).
  • Social presets: Instagram, Facebook OG, HD, Full HD, thumbnail, favicon.
  • Maintain aspect ratio: Prevent stretching.
  • Batch resize: Process many images at once.
  • Format conversion: JPEG, PNG, or WEBP output.
  • 100% private: All processing happens in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes, 100% free with no watermark, no signup, and no file limits.

Are my images uploaded?

No. All resizing happens locally in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

Yes. Upload multiple images and resize them all with the same dimensions in one click.

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Downscaling usually preserves quality well. Upscaling (making images larger) can appear softer because there are no new pixels to invent.
