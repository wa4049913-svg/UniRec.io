What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is a tool that counts the number of words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in your text. It's essential for writers, students, bloggers, and anyone who needs to meet specific word count requirements.

How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text in the box above
  2. See live counts update as you type
  3. Use the action buttons to copy, paste, or clear
  4. Check detailed statistics below the stats grid

Common Word Count Targets

  • Twitter/X Post: 280 characters max
  • Instagram Caption: 2,200 characters max
  • Blog Post: 1,000–2,000 words
  • SEO Article: 1,500–2,500 words
  • Essay (High School): 500–1,000 words
  • Essay (College): 1,500–3,000 words
  • Book Chapter: 2,000–5,000 words

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool save my text?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is calculated at 200 words per minute — the average adult reading speed. Speaking time is calculated at 130 WPM.

Is there a character limit?

No. You can paste text of any length. The counter updates instantly.
