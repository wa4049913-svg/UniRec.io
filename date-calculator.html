What is a Date Calculator?

A date calculator helps you work with dates — whether you need to find out how many days are between two dates, add days to a date (like calculating a deadline), or subtract days (like finding a past date).

How to Use

  1. Choose the mode: Days Between, Add Days, or Subtract Days
  2. Enter the date(s)
  3. Click the calculate button
  4. See the result instantly

Common Uses

  • Days Between: Countdown to an event, project duration, age in days
  • Add Days: Calculate deadlines, return dates, future appointments
  • Subtract Days: Find past dates, calculate invoice due dates

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate days between two dates?

Enter both dates in the "Days Between" tab. The calculator will show the exact number of days.

Does this include weekends?

Yes. The total days include weekends and holidays. For business days only, you'd need a separate business days calculator.

Can I calculate dates in the past?

Yes. Use the "Subtract Days" tab or enter past dates in the "Days Between" tab.
