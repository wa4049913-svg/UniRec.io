Your Exact Age

What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator tells you exactly how old you are based on your date of birth. Instead of just saying "25 years old," it breaks down your age into years, months, days, hours, and even minutes.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter your date of birth
  2. Optionally, change the "Calculate As Of" date
  3. Click Calculate My Age
  4. See your exact age instantly

Why Use This Age Calculator?

  • 100% Free — No signup, no payment
  • Accurate — Down to the minute
  • Private — Everything runs in your browser
  • Fast — Instant results
  • Mobile-Friendly — Works on any device

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this age calculator accurate?

Yes. It calculates your exact age down to the day, hour, and minute.

Does it store my information?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser. Your data is never sent anywhere.

Can I calculate my age on a future date?

Yes. Just change the "Calculate As Of" field to any future date.
