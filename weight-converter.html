What is a Weight Converter?

A weight converter helps you convert between different units of mass or weight — kilograms, pounds, ounces, grams, stones, and tons. It's useful for cooking, shipping, fitness, and international trade.

Common Weight Conversions

  • 1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds = 1000 grams
  • 1 pound = 0.453592 kilograms = 16 ounces
  • 1 ounce = 28.3495 grams
  • 1 stone = 6.35029 kilograms = 14 pounds
  • 1 metric ton = 1000 kilograms = 2204.62 pounds
  • 1 US ton = 907.185 kilograms = 2000 pounds

How to Use

  1. Enter the value you want to convert
  2. Select the "From" unit
  3. Select the "To" unit
  4. Click Convert
  5. Use the swap button (⇄) to reverse units
